## How the files were obtained

Found via unsplash.com search in a browser session; each photo page was opened to confirm the photographer and the free
license label; each file was downloaded once on 2026-09-30 from Unsplash's image CDN
(`https://images.unsplash.com/photo-<id>?w=1200&q=80&fm=webp&fit=max`, i.e. resized and re-encoded by Unsplash's CDN, with
no other modification). The photos show generic products; the catalog's brands and product names are fictional and
are not the brands or products in the photos. Photos showing a visible third-party brand mark were rejected during selection.
